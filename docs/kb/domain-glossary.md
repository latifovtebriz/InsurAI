# Domain Glossary

| Term | In Code | Definition & Business Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Sum Insured | `sum_insured` | Maximum payable limit under the policy for covered perils. |
| Deductible (Excess) | `deductible` | Fixed amount retained by insured; subtracted from payable claim amount. |
| Premium | `premium` | Cost of insurance risk coverage calculated deterministically. |
| FNOL | `fnol` | First Notice of Loss; initial intake of claim notification from customer. |
| Constructive Total Loss (CTL) | `total_loss` | Loss where repair cost exceeds threshold (default 70% of market value). |
| Pro-Rata | `prorata` | Linear daily apportionment of earned vs unearned premium. |
| Unearned Premium Reserve | `upr` | Portion of written premium corresponding to unexpired policy period. |
| IBNR | `ibnr` | Incurred But Not Reported reserve estimation for latent claims. |
| Subrogation | `subrogation` | Insurer's legal right to recover loss payout from liable third parties. |
| Four-Eyes Principle | `four_eyes` | Mandatory approval by a second authorized user for critical actions. |
